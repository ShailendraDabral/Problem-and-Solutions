# Remarks on the textbook and its answer key

(Plain-text version; the typeset version is the final appendix of the PDF. "Problem s-n" = problem n of Section s.)

## Misprints in problem statements or text

- **4-17:** "x= when t=t_1" is read as "x=x_1 when t=t_1" (consistent with the key).
- **33-8:** "Figure 33" should be Figure 39 (the sawtooth of series (11)).
- **35-16:** "cos^3x=(3/4)x + (1/4)cos 3x" should read (3/4) cos x+1/4cos 3x.
- **42-2(b):** the statement prints f(θ)=0, but the key's answer is the solution for f(θ)=θ; both are given.
- **42-3:** the Poisson kernel denominator "2R2cos" should be 2Rr cos(θ-φ).
- **43-6(a):** the equation should read Py"+P'y'+Ry=0 (a factor y is missing on the last term).
- **47, formula (5):** J_p'-(p/x)  J_p=+J_{p+1} should have the sign -J_{p+1} (as follows from formula (3), and as (6) requires).
- **49-5:** ε→∞ should be ε→0.
- **60-3:** "dx/dy" should be dx/dt; **61-6(c):** "dx/df" should be dx/dt.
- **Chapter 1 Miscellaneous 6:** the cross-reference "Problem 3-2" should be Problem 3-5. **Misc. 13** contains the printed typo "is is".
- **73, formula (5):** the relative error is (y-y_n)/y with a minus sign; the plus sign printed is a misprint (the text's own Table 1 confirms this).
- **73-6:** the problem has a solution at x=1 (y=tan x, since 1<π/2), contrary to the text.
- **75-6:** the local error of the three-term Taylor method is y"'h^3/6, not y"'h^3/12.
- **77-1, 77-2:** as printed these contain misprints ("y'(1)=0", "r=0.4", "x'=y"'); the interpretation used is stated in each solution.



## Differences from the answer key

- **4-7, 4-12, 4-19, 4-29:** the key's numbers (3531; 35.35%; 27.72 min; 3330, 3850, 10510, 7010 years) differ slightly from the exact values (3535.5; 35.36%; 27.73; 3323, 3833, 10502, 7016), because of intermediate rounding.
- **7-6(b):** the key gives n=3/4; n=-1/2 also separates the variables (both lead to the same solution).
- **10-9:** the key prints 8/10(10-t)^4; the coefficient must be 8/10^4 for x(0)=2.
- **11-3(b):** the key's family y=log(c_1e^x+e^{-x})+c_2 is incomplete; the complete family is y=log(Ae^x+Be^{-x}).
- **11-4 and 20-5:** the key's "about 89 minutes" and "about 90 minutes" are about 85 minutes for R=4000 miles, g=32 ft/s^2.
- **22-10:** the key's answer duplicates that of Problem 11 and does not satisfy the equation; the correct answer is (c_1+c_2x)cos ax+(c_3+c_4x)sin ax.
- **Chapter 2 Miscellaneous 46:** the key's relation should read c_1^2y=c_1x+log(c_1x-1)+c_2. **Misc. 11:** the key gives only one of three families.
- **35-15:** the key's signs in the cos^2x sine series are those of the sin^2x series; the correct signs are +.
- **51-8:** the key's entry labelled "8(a)" is the answer to part (b).
- **70-6(a):** the key's "all points with y_0 ≠ 0" is the answer for y'=|y|; for the printed equation y'=y|y| the answer is all points.

Where the key differs only by an arbitrary constant, a different but equivalent closed form, or a different (equally valid) particular solution, no remark is made in this list.
